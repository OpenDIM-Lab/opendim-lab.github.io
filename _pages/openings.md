# Join OpenDIM Lab

We welcome motivated researchers and students who are interested in robotics,
tactile sensing, dexterous manipulation, robot learning, and embodied
intelligence.

## Open positions

We welcome applications from:

- Postdoctoral researchers
- Ph.D. students
- Master's students
- Undergraduate students
- Research assistants

We also accept visiting students and research interns, subject to project
availability.

## Background and expectations

Applicants with a background in computer science, mechanical engineering and
manufacturing, automation, information engineering, or other
robotics-related fields are encouraged to apply. In-person collaboration is
preferred.

Please send a CV to
<a href="mailto:y.lin@bit.edu.cn">y.lin at bit.edu.cn</a>. Recruitment is
open on a rolling basis.
